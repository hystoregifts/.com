Frequently Asked Questions & Help Center

What are the Cash on Delivery (COD) rules?

COD is strictly available for shipping locations within the Punjab province. Orders placed outside Punjab must be paid via JazzCash or Easypaisa.

How do I track my order?

You will receive a unique tracking ID upon checkout (e.g., HY-123456). Enter it into our Track Order page for real-time delivery status.
